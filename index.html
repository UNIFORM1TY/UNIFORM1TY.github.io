How we build

Four rules, enforced.

These are not aspirations. They are the constraints the runtime is built to reject violations of.

01

One owner per truth

Coordination, product and runtime data never collapse into each other. Boundaries are enforced, not documented.

02

Evidence over assertion

Every claim carries a command, a result and a timestamp. Staging is not verification.

03

Separate by design

Systems compose through versioned contracts, never through a shared database.

04

Truth is versioned

History is immutable and append-only. Records are superseded, never silently overwritten.

Private by default. Verified by construction.
